──────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. Deploy:
   scp command-center-dashboard.html user@192.168.0.169:~/
   ssh user@192.168.0.169
   sudo cp command-center-dashboard.html /opt/r510-dashboard/index.html
   sudo ./deploy-dashboard.sh

Access: http://192.168.0.169:8421
